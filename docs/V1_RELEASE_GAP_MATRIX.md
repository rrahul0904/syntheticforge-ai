## Release boundary

The candidate is a secured single-node product release. It does not claim horizontal scaling, HIPAA/GDPR/SOC 2 certification, live certification for unexecuted connectors, successful external AI verification, or deployment. PostgreSQL control-plane persistence is not implemented; SQLite remains authoritative and startup rejects multi-worker or multi-replica production configuration.

## Stub and deferred-surface audit

No `TODO` or `FIXME` markers were found in the application, scripts, tests, or release docs. The `NotImplementedError` occurrences in `app/connectors/base.py` are abstract adapter methods implemented by concrete connector classes. Broad `except: pass` sites are parser/validation fallbacks that intentionally skip unsupported optional metadata; they are not mocked production connectors. Connector runtime limitations and their acceptance evidence are recorded separately above and in `CONNECTOR_CERTIFICATION.md`.
